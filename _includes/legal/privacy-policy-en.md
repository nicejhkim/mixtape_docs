# Old Mix Tape Privacy Policy

Effective: 3 November 2026

Old Mix Tape ("the app") has no accounts and collects no information that identifies you — no name, email, phone number, address, contacts or photos. It shows no ads, uses no advertising identifier, and sells no data. It does not send a usage log to a developer server.

What follows is everything the app handles.

## 1. Stays on your device

- **Stations**: the station name, logo address, and stream address are stored on the device. The first launch includes a few radio stations.
- **Tapes**: the title, length (15, 30, or 60 minutes), cassette picture, color tag, creation time, recorded stretches (station name, start time, length), and the recorded audio files are stored on the device. The playback position stays on the device too.
- None of this is sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app removes it. Deleting a tape removes that audio file from the device.

## 2. Connections made to play or display

- Playing or recording a station connects directly to that station's stream address. The server can see the IP address used for the connection, as with any stream request. The app does not store that IP and does not send a name or account with the request.
- A station with a logo address loads the image from that address to draw it on screen.
- Typefaces are downloaded from Google Fonts the first time they are needed, then kept on the device.

## 3. What is not collected

The app does not use crash reports, usage analytics, an advertising identifier, or a leaderboard. It does not collect a name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

## 4. Purpose and retention

On-device data is used only to pick a station again and to play a tape you recorded. It stays until you uninstall the app or delete the tape.

## 5. Processors

The developer does not engage a processor for analytics. Streams and logos are requested from the address in question, and font files come from Google Fonts.

- Google Fonts — <https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy>

## 6. Stopping collection

Uninstalling the app deletes stations, tapes, and audio files, and the app makes no further connections.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children, and no data is knowingly collected from them.

## 8. Your rights

No usage log is kept on a developer server. Data on the device is removed by uninstalling the app or deleting a tape. Questions can go to the address below.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and the change ships with a new app version.
