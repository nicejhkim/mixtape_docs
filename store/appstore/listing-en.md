## Description (4000 / 966)

```
An app for hearing internet radio and keeping the moment on a cassette tape.

■ Listening
· Station details come from radio-browser.info. Search there, or add a name and stream address yourself.
· Play the station you pick on a cassette player.
· If the stream drops, the screen says so right away.

■ Recording
· Before you record, pick the cassette, a title, a length (15, 30, or 60 minutes), and an optional color.
· Start and stop the recording yourself while the station plays.
· Recording stops when the tape is full.
· One tape can hold several takes, including other stations.
· If the station drops mid-recording, what you have up to that point is kept and recording stops.

■ Shelf
· Finished tapes play again from the shelf.
· You cannot cut or reorder a take. A tape can only be deleted whole.
· Up to four tapes can be kept for free.
· Nothing is sent out of the app.

■ No strings
· No ads.
· No in-app purchases.
· No account. Stations and tapes stay on this device.
```
